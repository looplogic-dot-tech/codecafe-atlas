CodeCafe Atlas v1.0.24.31 — Regression Report

Scope
-----
Introduce a bilingual software-use agreement and per-OS-user acceptance record
without changing, encrypting, deleting, or claiming ownership over licensee data.

Protected rules
---------------
1. Agreement appears before MainWindow and Database construction.
2. Spanish and English texts share one immutable agreement version.
3. Acceptance stores the SHA-256 of both canonical texts.
4. Each operating-system user accepts independently.
5. A changed version or hash requires acceptance again.
6. Acceptance writes atomically outside SQLite.
7. Declining exits without opening the operational database.
8. Licensee operational data remains licensee property and exportable.
9. Termination never deletes, encrypts, appropriates, or alters licensee data.
10. All v1.0.24.30 and earlier functionality remains protected.

Validation
----------
- Python compilation and AST validation.
- Bilingual clause and canonical-hash checks.
- Static startup-order and Help-menu integration checks.
- Full accumulated Atlas validators and identity scan.
- Document render and page-by-page visual inspection performed separately.
